About Breanna

A painter's eye.
An artisan's hand.

I am an artist who works across canvas, walls, and surfaces. Whether it is an original painting, a hand-applied decorative finish, or a custom mural — every piece starts with the same foundation: a trained eye for color, light, and composition.

I work directly with homeowners, interior designers, and architects throughout South Florida. The relationship is collaborative — I am personally on-site for every project, from first conversation through the final detail.

What I Do

Paintings, surfaces, and everything in between.

Every project is custom. Below are examples of what I offer — if you have something in mind that is not listed, reach out. I love a creative challenge.

01

Original Paintings

Oil on canvas, panel, or wall — botanicals, landscapes, portraits, abstracts, and anything in between. Commissioned pieces created specifically for your space.

02

Decorative Finishes

Venetian plaster, limewash, faux brick and stone, aged patinas, and more. Hand-applied finishes that give walls texture, depth, and character.

03

Murals & Custom Work

Large-scale painted murals, trompe l'oeil, and one-of-a-kind installations. Designed for your architecture and painted entirely by hand.

04

Retouching & Restoration

Seamless blending, blemish repair, and surface touch-ups — breathing new life into existing work or spaces that need a refined hand.

05

Inspired Recreations

Reimagined works from photographs or classic paintings — capturing what you love about an existing piece while adding an original, personal touch.

Common Questions

Before you reach out.

How do I know what is right for my space?

That is what the initial consultation is for — and it is complimentary. We will talk through your vision, look at your space, and I will recommend options that fit. No obligation.

Do you work with designers and architects?

Absolutely. A large portion of my work is collaborative — I attend site meetings, work from your specs and mood boards, and coordinate with your timeline. Let's connect.

What does a project typically cost?

Every project is custom, so pricing varies. I provide a detailed proposal after our initial conversation — no obligation, no pressure.

How far in advance should I book?

I take on a limited number of projects to give each one my full attention. The earlier we connect, the more flexibility we have. Check current availability.
